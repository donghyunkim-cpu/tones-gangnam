Six ways to tighten tissue, each reaching a different layer. Choosing between them is a question about your skin, not about which machine is newest.

When the problem is not missing structure but tissue that has loosened, filler cannot fix it. Energy can — provided it is aimed at the layer that is actually failing.

Tightening is a question of depth, not of power.

The idea

Lifting happens at a specific depth

Creams reach the top layer. Needles reach the fat and the bone. The layer that actually holds the face up — the fibrous sheet beneath the fat — can only be reached by focused energy, and only if the device is aimed at it deliberately.

Choosing

Start from the complaint, not the machine

Patients usually arrive having already chosen a device from a friend or an advertisement. This is how the choice is actually made.

"My jawline has blurred."Ultrasound at the SMAS, because that is the layer letting go. Thread lifting if it has to show this week.
"My skin looks tired and crepey."Radiofrequency in the dermis — Thermage or Density, depending on how your skin behaves.
"I have a wedding in three weeks."Thread lifting, or filler. The energy devices peak at month three, which is after your event.
"I cannot look like I have had anything done."Titanium. Three wavelengths in one pass, nothing visible on the day, and the change arrives slowly enough that nobody places it.
"My arms and stomach, not my face."Onda. It is the one on this list designed to work on body contour as well.
"My face looks hollow, not saggy."None of these. That is a structure problem — see filler design.

Questions

Before you ask us

Ask Dr. Yang directly →
Is it painful?+

Focused ultrasound is felt as brief deep heat, strongest along the jaw and over bone. Radiofrequency is warmer but gentler. Thread lifting is done under local anaesthesia. Settings are adjusted to what you can tolerate — a session you endure at maximum power is not a better session.

Will I lose facial fat?+

Not when energy is aimed correctly. Hollowing happens when shots land in the fat pad instead of the target layer, which is exactly what confirming the depth on screen is for.

When will I see it?+

Ultrasound and radiofrequency work by rebuilding collagen, so the change arrives gradually and peaks around month three. Thread lifting is the exception — that one lifts on the day. Anyone promising a permanent result from a single energy session is overselling it.

Can I fly the next day?+

After ultrasound or radiofrequency, yes — often the same evening. After thread lifting, allow a little more time and avoid pressure on the treated area, including how you sleep on a plane.

Device or filler, if I can only do one?+

If you need the change visible this week, filler or threads. If you are treating the direction your face is heading rather than today's photograph, energy. Many patients do both in one visit, device first.
