Questions

Asked before booking a flight

Ask Dr. Yang directly →
How many days do I actually need?+

Two nights for filler, injectables or a lifting device. Four if you want a laser course compressed into one trip. One night is possible but leaves no room if a flight is delayed.

Can everything be done in one visit?+

Usually yes, in the right order — device first, then filler, laser last. What we will not do is stack boosters and botulinum in the same area on the same day, because a reaction then cannot be attributed.

What if something goes wrong after I leave?+

Message the clinic line first — most concerns at day three are ordinary swelling and settle. If something needs hands-on care, you get a written summary for a local doctor the same day, and Dr. Yang will speak to them if they want to call.

Is there a deposit?+

No. You reserve a slot by agreeing to the plan, and pay after treatment. Cancelling costs nothing — we would rather hold an empty hour than take money for a visit you are unsure about.

Do you work with medical tourism agencies?+

No. An agency puts someone between you and the doctor, and the cost reaches your bill either way. Please contact us directly.
