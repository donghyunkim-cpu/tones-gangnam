What we will not do

Four things that would make our reviews worth less

Paying for them

No discount, credit or free session is offered in exchange for writing a review. A review you were paid for is an advertisement.

Filtering them

We do not ask only satisfied patients to post, and we do not request removal of a review because it is critical.

Retouching photographs

No smoothing, reshaping or relighting between before and after. If a result needed editing to be visible, it was not a result.

Promising an outcome

No treatment here is guaranteed to reproduce another person's result. Anatomy, age and healing differ, and so does what is achievable.

Set your expectations

Three things worth knowing in advance

Better raised here than discovered on the day. If any of these is a problem for you, we would rather you found out before you travelled.

You may be talked out of what you came for

If the change you want needs surgery, or old product removed first, Dr. Yang will say so rather than proceed. Some patients leave without treatment.

The first result is deliberately conservative

Under-treating is reversible; over-treating takes months to undo. The review appointment exists to add if it is needed, and it is the one people most often skip.

Seen by the head doctor himself

Every consultation and treatment is Dr. Yang's own, so appointments are finite. Send your dates early if you are travelling.

Judge the plan,not the website.

Send three photos. You get back the points Dr. Yang would treat, the approach and the time it needs — at no cost and with nothing booked. Fees are confirmed when you visit.
