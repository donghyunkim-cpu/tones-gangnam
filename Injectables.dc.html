The dosing rule

Start under. Review at two weeks. Add if it is needed.

Too much botulinum cannot be undone — it has to wear off. So the first dose is deliberately conservative, and a review appointment is built into the plan.

Recommended for

Which one is actually yours

Bring the complaint, not the product name. Most people arrive asking for botox and leave with a different plan.

"Lines show even when my face is still"Filler or boosters — botulinum only stops lines that fold with movement.
"I look angry in photographs"Glabella and brow-tail botulinum, a handful of units per side.
"My jaw looks square from the front"Masseter, if the muscle bulges when you clench. Bone width does not respond.
"My skin looks dull and tight"A booster course, timed two weeks apart, before any shaping work.
"I want fuller lips, but subtle"Border definition with a soft product, under half a syringe in most cases.

Points to know

Small volumes, strict habits

Units are counted out loud

You are told the dose per site before it is drawn up, and it goes on your chart for next time.

One vial, one patient

Reconstituted in front of you. No shared vials, no leftovers carried to the next appointment.

A two-week review is included

In person if you are still in Seoul, by photo if you have flown home. Touch-ups inside that window are free.

Nothing is stacked on the day

Boosters and botulinum in the same area are separated by at least a week so a reaction can be attributed.

Questions

The ones we hear most

Ask Dr. Yang directly →
Will my face look frozen?+

Not at the doses used here. The aim is to weaken the pull, not switch the muscle off, so you keep a brow that lifts and a smile that reaches the eyes. If you have been frozen before, tell us — it usually means the dose was too high or placed too centrally.

Can I do this the day I land?+

Yes. There is no downtime and no dressing, and you can wear makeup after four hours. We only ask that you skip alcohol that evening and stay upright for four hours after botulinum.

Do I have to keep doing it forever?+

No. Everything wears off and the face returns to where it would have been — it does not get worse for having been treated. Many patients treat before an event and then leave it for a year.

Will bruising show?+

Botulinum rarely bruises. Lip filler and boosters can leave small pinpoint marks for two to four days, coverable with makeup. Stop fish oil, high-dose vitamin E and aspirin-type painkillers for three days beforehand if you can.

Can I combine this with filler lifting?+

Often, and in that order — structure first, then expression. Both can be done in the same visit when the areas do not overlap, which is what most travelling patients choose.
