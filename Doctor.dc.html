How he practises

Why the list is short

A face is read before it is treated. Most of a consultation is spent deciding where product should go — and whether it should go at all.

The same volume of filler produces very different results depending on where it is placed. Two faces that look alike from the front can need opposite plans, which is why the design step takes longer than the injection itself.

The clinic works within a defined set of treatments: filler design, injectables, lifting and tightening, Metacell and laser. No surgery. When a concern falls outside what these can do, Dr. Yang says so at the consultation instead of substituting something that will not solve it.

Many patients arrive having had treatment elsewhere — some of it good, some of it visible. The usual request is not for more, but for someone to look at the whole face and do less.

How he works

Three rules, andone thing he refuses.

01

Look before you plan

Upright, in motion, and in photographs. Half of what patients call sagging is asymmetry they have had since twenty, and treating it as sagging makes it worse.

02

The least that will hold

Under-treat, review at two weeks, add if needed. You cannot take back a face that has been overfilled without dissolving months of work.

03

Nothing irreversible

Hyaluronic acid only, with hyaluronidase in the room. If a result is wrong, it should be possible to undo it the same week — not live with it for years.

And one refusal

One plan, one doctor

The plan and its fee are explained in full at the clinic before anything begins, and nothing starts until you agree to it. If a follow-up session turns out to be unnecessary, it is not carried out.
