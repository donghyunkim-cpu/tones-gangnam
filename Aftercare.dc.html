Aftercare

What to expect, and what to avoid

Clinic guidance

Normal, and temporary

Redness, warmth, swelling, bruising, tenderness, tightness or general sensitivity may appear for a short period after treatment. All of these settle on their own.

How strong they are and how long they last depends on your skin, the area treated and the method used — which is why the figures we give you are ranges rather than promises.

For the first days

—Do not rub or massage the treated area firmly.
—No heavy exercise, alcohol or smoking on the day of treatment.
—Avoid saunas, jjimjilbang and hot baths for a period after treatment.
—No scrubs, peels or strong facial massage until the skin has settled.
—Use sunscreen when you go out, and keep the skin well moisturised.
—After thread lifting, take particular care not to press or strain the treated area.

If you experience severe pain, swelling or redness that does not subside, or any change in skin colour, contact the clinic and speak to a doctor — from wherever you are.

Reactions and recovery time differ according to individual skin condition, the area treated and the method used. Your own precautions and recovery period will be explained by the medical team for the treatment you actually have.
